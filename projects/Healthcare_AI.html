Healthcare AI

Advanced CNN-based breast cancer detection system achieving 94.5% accuracy with automated screening capabilities for early diagnosis.

Project Overview

The Healthcare AI project implements a state-of-the-art Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for detecting breast cancer from medical imaging data. Trained on large-scale datasets, the model identifies malignant patterns with high precision and recall.

Deployed via Streamlit, it provides an intuitive interface for medical professionals to upload images and receive instant predictions, significantly reducing diagnostic time.

Key Metrics

Detection Accuracy 94.5%
Sensitivity Rate 96%
Specificity 92%
Processing Speed < 5 seconds

The Problem

  • Early Detection Challenges

    Early detection challenges in medical diagnosis requires expert radiologists to analyze thousands of images annually.

  • Limited Expert Availability

    Limited accessibility to expert pathologists in developing regions affects patient outcomes and healthcare equity.

  • Time-Consuming Analysis

    Manual image analysis is time-consuming and prone to human error, leading to diagnostic delays.
