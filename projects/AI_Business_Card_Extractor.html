AI Business Card Extractor

An intelligent system leveraging OCR and AI to automatically extract and structure business card data with high accuracy and reliability.

Project Overview

The AI Business Card Extractor is a full-stack web application that automates the process of extracting contact information from business card images. By combining Tesseract OCR for text extraction with Google Gemini AI for intelligent data structuring, the system delivers accurate, properly formatted contact information ready for database storage.

The backend is built with FastAPI for high performance, while the user-friendly Streamlit interface provides an intuitive experience. MongoDB stores user data and extraction history, enabling multi-user support and data persistence.

The Problem

  • Manual Data Entry Bottleneck

    Business professionals and organizations spend hours manually entering contact information from business cards into CRM systems and databases.

  • OCR Quality Inconsistency

    Traditional OCR solutions struggle with variations in card layouts, fonts, and backgrounds, resulting in garbled or incomplete text extraction.

  • Data Structuring Challenge

    Even with good OCR results, intelligently categorizing and structuring diverse card formats (name, email, phone, company) requires manual intervention.

  • Lack of Scalability

    Organizations processing bulk business cards lack a scalable, automated solution with data persistence and user management.

Core Features
